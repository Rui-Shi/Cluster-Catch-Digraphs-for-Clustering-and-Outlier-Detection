## Design recap

One-factor-at-a-time ablation of `nnccd.radi()`, the per-point radius search
inside `nnccd_clustering_quantile()` that `SUNMCCD_outlier()` calls. Each
toggle is varied alone against the `(both, on, ascend)` baseline (`stock`,
which reproduces published SUN-MCCD bit-for-bit — verified by the smoke
run's `identical()` checks on score, radii, and cluster assignment):

| variant_id | NND statistic | centre removed | search direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stock` | both (either mean or median below its quantile rejects CSR) | yes | ascend |
| `stat_mean` | mean only | yes | ascend |
| `stat_median` | median only | yes | ascend |
| `centre_off` | both | no | ascend |
| `direction_descend` | both | yes | descend |

4 settings (`{uniform, gaussian} × {d=3, d=10}`, n = 200 nominal, 5%
contamination, S_min = 0.05, low.num = 3, α resolved per d by
`nn_quant_label_paper_SUN`) × 5 variants × 100 reps, paired by seed
(`seed = 9100 + rep_id`, identical data draw across all 5 variants at a
given (setting, rep)).

## Plain reading

- **NND statistic (`both` vs `mean`-only vs `median`-only).** In the
  uniform settings, the BA and F2 paired differences between `stat_mean` /
  `stat_median` and `stock` are all under 0.003 in absolute mean, and the
  reps split close to evenly between "variant worse" and "variant better."
  In the Gaussian settings, `stat_median` differs from `stock` by -0.0076
  BA / -0.0390 F2 (d=3) and -0.0076 BA / -0.0357 F2 (d=10), with the
  variant above stock in 66/100 (d=3) and 62/100 (d=10) reps on F2.
  `stat_mean` differs from `stock` by -0.0049 BA / -0.0248 F2 (d=3) and
  -0.0016 BA / -0.0075 F2 (d=10), variant above stock in 54/100 (d=3) and
  20/100 (d=10) reps on F2. Direction is consistent across both Gaussian
  settings for both single-statistic variants: the single-statistic
  variants outscore the paper's `both` rule there.

- **Centre-point removal (`on` vs `off`).** In the uniform settings the
  paired differences are under 0.0003 in absolute mean, split close to
  evenly. In the Gaussian settings `centre_off` differs from `stock` by
  0.0022 BA / 0.0107 F2 (d=3) and 0.0041 BA / 0.0171 F2 (d=10), with
  `stock` above `centre_off` in 40/100 (d=3) and 48/100 (d=10) reps on F2
  versus `centre_off` above `stock` in 29/100 (d=3) and 27/100 (d=10).
  Direction is consistent across both Gaussian settings: removing the
  centre point (the paper's choice) outscores including it.

- **Search direction (`ascend` vs `descend`).** In the uniform settings
  the paired differences are under 0.002 in absolute mean. In the Gaussian
  settings `direction_descend` differs from `stock` by -0.0132 BA /
  -0.0739 F2 (d=3) and -0.0121 BA / -0.0578 F2 (d=10), with the variant
  above stock in 85/100 (d=3) and 73/100 (d=10) reps on F2 — the largest
  magnitude of the three toggles.

- **Mechanism visibility.** `mean_radius` differs across all 5 variants at
  every one of the 4 settings (e.g. uniform_d3: stock 0.571, stat_mean
  0.594, stat_median 0.640, centre_off 0.537, direction_descend 0.661),
  confirming each toggle changes the constructed balls, not only the
  downstream score. `n_clusters` is 2 with sd 0 in all 20 (setting,
  variant) cells — every method finds the 2 generated clusters in every
  rep at every setting. `n_error` is 0 in all 2000 cells.

- **Pattern across toggles.** All three toggles show near-zero paired
  differences in the uniform settings and larger, directionally consistent
  differences in the Gaussian settings, in this rank order by F2 magnitude
  in the Gaussian settings: search direction (-0.058 to -0.074) > NND
  statistic, median (-0.036 to -0.039) > NND statistic, mean (-0.008 to
  -0.025) > centre removal (+0.011 to +0.017). The three components that
  move the score at all in this design are the ones separating `both` from
  a single statistic, `on` from `off` centre removal, and `ascend` from
  `descend`, and all three move it in the Gaussian settings only.

## Caveats

1. **Approximate null for `centre_off`.** The Monte Carlo envelope
   (`NNDest.simpois.lower.quant()`) simulates i.i.d. CSR clouds. With
   centre removal on, the observed cloud is a genuinely free point set,
   matching that null. With centre removal off, one of the j (or `size`)
   observed points is not free — it sits, by construction, at the ball's
   own centre, since that is the point the ball was grown around.
   Reading the size-matched envelope entry (`WP9_PROTOCOL.md`, "centre-off
   indexing decision") corrects the point count but not this placement
   constraint, so the j-point i.i.d. CSR envelope is an approximate null
   for `centre_off`, not an exact one. Its results above should be read
   with that in mind.

2. **Holm-correction toggle dropped, not measured here.** For two
   hypotheses (mean, median), Holm's step-down procedure reduces to
   comparing the smaller p-value against α/2 — for the code's either/or
   rejection rule, this is the same decision as running the existing rule
   at the α/2 quantile table. That α/2 table exists at d=3 (α/2 = 5%,
   suffix `95`) but not at d=10 (α/2 = 0.05%, no `9995`-suffixed table was
   ever generated). The effect of halving α is already measured directly
   by the WP2(a) α sweep (`42_wp2a_alpha_sweep.R`), which varies α across
   the same marginal-quantile tables this method uses, so a dedicated Holm
   toggle here would be redundant with that sweep rather than a fourth
   ablation arm this driver is missing.
